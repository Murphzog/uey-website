Students shaping their city

Urban Education for Youth is a school-visit program run by Urban Systems in Bend, Oregon.

Why it exists

Kids rarely get a say in how their city is planned. UEY gives them one.

  • High schoolers teach

    Volunteers visit an elementary classroom with their friends, because kids listen to older kids.

  • Kids share ideas

    Students say what they want from their streets and parks, because they know their neighborhood.

  • The city hears them

    Urban Systems brings those ideas into real planning projects, so their voice shapes Bend.

Urban Systems

An urban research organization dedicated to developing integral systems to make the urban planning process more efficient.

Urban Systems develops low-cost traffic sensors and digital twins for urban planning.

We run UEY because the kids in today's classrooms are the people who will live in tomorrow's city. Plans being made now will shape their streets, schools and neighborhoods for decades, so their ideas belong in the process from the start.
