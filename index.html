How a visit works

Low time, done with friends.

  1. 01Pick your groupGo with your own friends. You can ask for a sibling's classroom.
  2. 02Get a short briefing10 to 15 minutes before each visit. Your Group Lead brings everyone.
  3. 03Teach one classLeave school for the visit and run a short lesson and activity with elementary students.
  4. 04Bring the ideas backWhat the class says feeds into real city planning projects.
  5. 05Hang out afterA thank-you hangout for everyone who visited.

For volunteers

Community service hours, something real for your résumé, and a day out of class.

  • LeadGroup LeadCoordinates their own friend group: brings them to the briefing, then presents with them.
  • JoinVolunteerJoins a Group Lead's group for one or more visits.
