For high school students

Bring your friends

A 10 to 15 minute briefing, a classroom visit with your own group, and a thank-you hangout after. What the kids say goes into real city planning in Bend.

Express interest

What you get

  • Volunteer hours

    Community service hours, plus real planning work you can put on a résumé.

  • Go with your friends

    You pick who you go with. You can ask for a sibling's classroom.

  • A day out of class

    Leave school to teach a short lesson and run an activity.

  • Free Food Follow-up Meeting

    Free food and drinks for everyone who visited.

How it works

  1. 1

    First meeting

    Meet the team and pick your group. Start one as a Group Lead, or join a friend's.

  2. 2

    Second meeting

    A 10 to 15 minute briefing on the lesson you'll teach.

  3. 3

    The day off from school

    Visit a classroom with your group, teach the lesson and run the activity.

  4. 4

    Free Food Follow-up Meeting

    Free food and drinks for everyone who visited.
