Who is UEY for?

  • Elementary schools

    Kindergarten to 5th grade classrooms that host a visit and share ideas about their own city.

  • Middle schools

    6th to 8th grade classes that host a visit, with a lesson matched to their grade.

  • High schools

    9th to 12th graders who teach the visits as volunteers and Group Leads.

What a visit looks like

  1. 1

    A group arrives

    High school volunteers led by their Group Lead.

  2. 2

    A preplanned lesson

    Tailored to your class's needs.

  3. 3

    Ideas that go somewhere

    What your class says goes to your city's planning organization.
